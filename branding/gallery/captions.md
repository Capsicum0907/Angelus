# Gallery

Upload in this order; the first is the card image.

## 01-out-over-nothing.png
**Out over nothing**

Three of them stepping off the cliff into open air, with nothing underneath. The next one goes wherever you are looking.

崖から空中へ3個。下には何もありません。次の1個は目線の先に現れます。

## 02-placed-against-nothing.png
**Placed against nothing**

One block at the end of the reach, placed with the crosshair on empty air. The frame is open, so the ground behind shows through.

届く距離いっぱいに置いた1個。枠の中は空いているので後ろの地面が透けます。

## 03-into-the-waterfall.png
**Into the waterfall**

It goes into falling water as readily as into open air.

空気だけでなく滝の中にも置けます。

## 04-across-the-valley.png
**Across the valley**

A crossing between two peaks, one block at a time. Break each one as you pass and it goes back into your inventory.

2つの峰の間を1個ずつ架けたところ。渡りながら壊せばインベントリに戻ります。

## 05-the-recipe.png
**The recipe**

Four feathers and four sticks make one angel block.

羽根4枚と棒4本で1つ作れます。
